# Brand Identity & Strategy: ikeen

> **Brand Name:** `ikeen` (styled lowercase as **ikeen**, or CamelCase as **iKeen** in technical specs)  
> **Tagline:** *Your Mind, Attuned.*  
> **Alternate Tagline:** *The Attentive Second Brain.*  
> **Brand Vision:** An agentic personal operating system that doesn't just store what you think—it anticipates what you need, observes your daily rhythm with acute clarity, and keeps you effortlessly focused.  
> **Author & Creator:** Dev Lee (Lesly Chuo)  
> **Date:** September 2026  

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Core Meaning

The name **ikeen** is built on two interlocked declarations:

1. **Personal Declaration ("I am keen"):** *Keen* denotes being eager, sharp, intellectually curious, and acutely perceptive. Saying *iKeen / ikeen* is an active, first-person declaration of ambition, mental clarity, and readiness to build and achieve.
2. **The Intelligent Companion ("iKeen"):** The vigilant, proactive companion that stays observant in the background—keeping a keen eye on your habits, tracking your runway, transcribing your spoken thoughts in sub-300ms, and orchestrating your day with bespoke intelligence.

### The Brand Paradox
* **Calm on the surface** (no notification spam, zero cognitive clutter, distraction-free markdown canvas).
* **Fiercely capable underneath** (multi-agent routing, durable Inngest cron jobs, vector semantic search, instant voice intelligence).

---

## 2. Voice, Tone & Persona

ikeen speaks like a world-class chief of staff combined with an elite systems architect:

* **Attentive, not intrusive:** Never shouts. Speaks when spoken to, or when scheduled routines deliver high-value morning briefings.
* **Precise and jargon-light:** Describes real-world outcomes and metrics rather than tech buzzwords.
* **Calmly confident:** Measured, reassuring, and grounded.

```
❌ Bad (Generic AI): 
"Hey there superstar! 🎉 Just checking in to see if you crushed your goals today! Ready to dive in?!"

✅ Good (ikeen Voice): 
"Morning, Lesly. You have 3 focused blocks today. Legs this afternoon, runway steady at 14 months. Ready when you are."
```

## 4. Typography & Spatial Layout

* **Display / Brand Wordmark:** `Geist Sans` or `SF Pro Display` (ultra-clean, modern geometric sans).
* **Body & Notes Canvas:** `Inter` or `System Sans` with tabular figures (`tabular-nums`) for habit counters and dates.
* **Agentic Terminal / Code / Data:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Geist Mono` for structured tool outputs, runway math, and slash commands.

### The Wordmark
```
   i k e e n .
```
* Set in lowercase geometric sans.
* The dot in the `i` features a subtle **Iris/Cyan radiant gradient** or glowing pulse, representing the watchful eye of the companion.

---

## 5. App Icon & Symbolism

### The Concept: "The Attuned Aperture"
* **Silhouette:** A dark titanium rounded squircle with Apple standard continuous corner radius.
* **Inner Glyph:** An abstract fusion of a lowercase **`i`** and an observant focal aperture/spark:
  * A central luminous core (the dot of the `i` / ambient microphone lens).
  * Two sleek, curved dynamic arcs framing it (representing the double 'ee' in keen, resembling acoustic waves or neural synapses).
* **Motion / Live Activity:** On iOS Dynamic Island and Apple Watch, the center dot gently breathes with a cyan halo when audio capture is active.
